Project case study

Problem

CSV transaction records can be cumbersome to scan for order totals, product mixes, and monthly patterns.

Objective

Calculate operational sales measures and present assumptions alongside the results.

Workflow

Load synthetic sample or local CSV → validate each record → filter, aggregate, export, and review.

Tools

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, File API, CSV parser, and inline SVG charts.

Output

Distinct order metrics, product rankings, monthly revenue, business insights, and filtered CSV export.

Limitations

Currency is not inferred. No tax, refunds, discounts, costs, or customer data are included. Trends are descriptive, not predictive.
